What Is Football?

Football, known as soccer in the USA, is the most popular sport in the world. Two teams of eleven players compete on a rectangular pitch, each trying to score by putting the ball into the opposing goal. Matches last ninety minutes, split into two forty-five minute halves, and the team with the most goals at the final whistle wins. Played by millions of people from city streets to enormous stadiums, the sport needs very little equipment, which is a big reason it has spread so widely.

Key Things to Know

  • Each team fields eleven players, including one goalkeeper.
  • Only the goalkeeper can handle the ball inside the penalty area.
  • A standard match lasts ninety minutes plus stoppage time.
  • The World Cup takes place every four years.
  • Referees enforce the laws of the game and show yellow and red cards.
